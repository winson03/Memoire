Save to Mémoire

Connect this browser to your private library.

Update available:
  1. Download the new version and unzip it.
  2. Replace your memoire-save folder with the new one.
  3. Open chrome://extensions and press ↻ on Save to Mémoire.
  4. Reload your X / Patreon tabs.

Loaded the extension from your Mémoire project folder instead? Then just press ↻.

Get your key from the “Save from web” page
  • X: press Save under any post with photos or videos.
  • Patreon: press Save on a post card, or Save to Mémoire on a post page.
  • Anywhere else: right-click a photo or video → Save to Mémoire, or click the toolbar icon to save the page’s post.
